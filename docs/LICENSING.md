# Licensing guide

The [LICENSE](../LICENSE) contains the governing terms. This page is a plain-language summary, not a replacement for those terms.

| Activity | Allowed? |
| --- | --- |
| Use the app personally, at school, or at work | Yes |
| Use it to prepare music for paid DJ gigs | Yes |
| Fork, modify, and share the app for free | Yes, with the license and copyright notices |
| Propose improvements through pull requests | Yes; the maintainer reviews what enters the official repository |
| Sell the app, a renamed copy, or a modified version | No, unless you obtain written permission |
| Charge for downloads, subscriptions, or access to the app | No, unless you obtain written permission |
| Include the app as part of a paid software product | No, unless you obtain written permission |
| Charge for optional training or support while the app remains freely available | Yes, subject to the license |

The project is **source-available**. Its no-sale restriction means it does not meet the [Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd). “Noncommercial use only” would be inaccurate: professional and business use are explicitly permitted.

The license does not grant access to provider services or rights to third-party music. Those remain governed by their own terms.

For the maintainer: this is custom license language, not a standard license or a legal opinion. Have qualified counsel review it before relying on it for enforcement. No particular enforceability or GitHub license-detection result is guaranteed.
