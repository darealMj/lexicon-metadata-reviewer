Test dataset

Starter labels are examples, not verified ground truth. Edit them to match your tagging policy. Expected tags, album titles, and release years are never sent to the model. Album/year checks appear separately; null expected_tags skips tag scoring.

Only artist and title are sent to the configured provider. One request per track per model, maximum 50 tracks × 3 models. Duplicate model IDs run only once. Paid models may incur charges. Every track is attempted, including after errors; no retries or cache.

Discover a better model

Randomly sample up to three eligible models, including your current model when it fits the filters. Preview first; no paid inference starts until you confirm the selected models.

Random runs require a dedicated OpenRouter key with a non-resetting credit limit, remaining credit no higher than this budget, and BYOK included in that limit. Set this in OpenRouter key settings. This cap also covers search; provider enforcement applies. Preview is read-only.

Automatic selection requires 20+ tracks, 5+ artists, at least 15 training and 5 holdout tracks, and strong scores on both splits. Seven starter tracks produce provisional rankings only. Selected winners become Model 1 for future lab runs; this does not add AI writes to the library.

F1 measures tag agreement using your aliases and acceptable extras. Unreviewed extras are shown for review and still reduce precision until approved in the dataset. Confidence is the model’s own estimate, not verified accuracy. Brier error compares reported confidence with your labels for suggested tags only; lower is better. Missing tags affect recall and F1.
